Ath. að $\angle EAC=\angle CBD$ Þar sem þau eru ferilhorn sem spanna sama boga. Höfum af reglu Ceva að $AC$, $BM$, og $FE$ skerast öll ís sama punkti e.o.a.e. $\frac{AB}{AF}\frac{FM}{MC}\frac{CE}{BE}=1$. Fáum:
\begin{align*}
MF=MC & \Leftrightarrow \frac{AB}{AF}\frac{CE}{BE}=1 \\
 & \Leftrightarrow AE || FC \\
 & \Leftrightarrow \angle FCA =\angle CAE \\
 & \Leftrightarrow \angle MCD =\angle CBD \\
 & \Leftrightarrow \triangle BMC \sim \triangle CMD \\
 & \Leftrightarrow \frac{MC}{MD}=\frac{MB}{MC} \\
 & \Leftrightarrow MC^2=MB\cdot MD
\end{align*}
